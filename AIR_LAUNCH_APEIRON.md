## Coverage

- Historical lineage: Bell X-1, X-15, Pegasus, SpaceShipOne and the Delta-class program.
- Carrier geometry, release at approximately 13.7 km, rocket boost, ballistic coast, microgravity, feathered return and runway recovery.
- Atmospheric density versus gravity; suborbital altitude versus orbital speed.
- Carrier center-wing load paths, concentrated attachment loads, separation transients, cabin pressurization, composite joints, fatigue and inspection.
- The 2014 SpaceShipTwo accident and the NTSB's human-error protection findings.
- Explicit separation of measured heritage, company targets and illustrative teaching calculations.
- Five classroom problems with explanations and numerical checks.
