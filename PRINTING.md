# Printing and fabrication checklist

[Course home](README.md) · [Syllabus](SYLLABUS.md) · [Project guide](PROJECT.md)

This public checklist summarizes the syllabus. It does **not** replace ELab orientation, machine-specific training, the full course printing guide or print-readiness approval. Detailed operating settings and the complete machine workflow are provided in Canvas and by lab staff.

## Before reserving or printing

- [ ] Complete the required agreement, orientation and machine-specific training.
- [ ] Use only your approved team slot, assigned printer and required supervision. Reservations are in Canvas; other courses share the printers.
- [ ] Use course-approved regular **1.75 mm PLA Pro** and the assigned printer/nozzle profile. Do not substitute materials without approval.
- [ ] Run **Coupon Only** in the project notebook, print the fit coupon and record the measured rod fit.
- [ ] For **Final Wing**, complete the physical coupon record and increment the revision. A digital pass does not replace the physical fit check.
- [ ] Inspect all STL/3MF modules in the assigned slicer at **millimetres, 100% scale**. Check dimensions, orientation, supports, interfaces and every layer.
- [ ] Save the slicer project, verify mass/time estimates and obtain print-readiness approval.

## During and after the approved slot

- [ ] Follow staff instructions for the bed, filament and machine checks. Supervise the first layers and confirm adhesion and extrusion.
- [ ] Do not operate alone, bypass alarms/guards or change another team's job. Stop and notify staff if conditions appear unsafe.
- [ ] Record printer, material, settings, times, failures and corrections.
- [ ] Let parts cool and remove them as trained. Label, weigh and inspect the printed set; clean the station.
- [ ] Dry-fit modules and rods before adhesive. Use epoxy only when assigned, under supervision and with the required controls and cure guidance.
- [ ] Document dimensions, interface fit, alignment, photographs and limitations. Obtain approval before a reprint.

The baseline final printed-wing budget is **300 g** of approved PLA Pro, plus course-issued rods and assigned assembly consumables. Personal material purchases are not required.

The printer room is **Engineering Laboratory, Room 104 / 104A**. Door access information is issued privately after training and is never published in this repository.
