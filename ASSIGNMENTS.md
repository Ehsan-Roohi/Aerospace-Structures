## Before submitting

- [ ] Include readable analytical solutions and an Engineering Judgment Record for the instructor-designated problem.
- [ ] State the system, load path, prediction, model, assumptions, boundary conditions and limitations.
- [ ] Include at least **two independent checks** for the designated problem: dimensions, equilibrium, a limiting case, an alternate method or a physical benchmark.
- [ ] Include source/notebook and a short README when code is assigned.
- [ ] Submit one **Claim–Evidence–Check slide**, both `.pptx` and exported `.pdf`, with confidence and the principal limitation. Use a synthesis, not a screenshot collage.
- [ ] Add a process and AI-use note: tools, contribution, changes, errors found and verification. “No generative AI used” is valid.
- [ ] Check Canvas for the deadline and submission destination. Do not put graded work in this public repository.

## One-slide brief and oral check

Everyone prepares a slide for every homework. A preassigned rotation of about 10–11 students completes a **90-second explanation plus one short question** after each deadline, in a 20–25-minute workshop block. Across six homeworks, each student completes one live oral check. The rotation is posted by Week 3.

A narrated recording is an approved absence/accessibility alternative, not a routine requirement for all six assignments. The oral check is a pass/revise authenticity gate.

| Homework rubric | Weight within homework |
|---|---:|
| Engineering judgment and transparent reasoning | 40% |
| Mechanics and numerical correctness | 25% |
| Independent verification or assigned AI audit | 20% |
| One-slide communication | 10% |
| Reproducibility, attribution and AI disclosure | 5% |

## Midterm and extensions

The individual midterm is **October 27**, worth **17.5%**, equivalent to two homework units. Generative AI and unauthorized online assistance are not permitted. The formula-sheet policy and sample format will be posted at least one week before the exam.

The syllabus provides two 48-hour individual homework extension passes, activated in writing before the deadline. They do not apply to the midterm, oral rotation, project safety gates, printer reservations or final presentation. See [all deadline and accommodation provisions](SYLLABUS.md#deadlines-and-extensions) before using a pass.
