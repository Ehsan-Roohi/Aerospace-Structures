Method, geometry & evidence

This is a three-dimensional surface-of-revolution calculation. Constant-source conical ring bands solve the Laplace no-penetration condition. A damped nonlinear shape iteration makes the cavity tangential speed constant. Bernoulli gives σ=(qc/U∞)²−1 and p∞=pc+½ρU∞²σ.

The front disk at x=0 is physical. The downstream disk at x=L is an artificial Riabouchinsky image used to close a symmetric equivalent body. L is the full separation, not half-cavity length. Its cancelling force is not subtracted from the physical front-disk drag.

Every cavity band is checked at its fitted midpoint and at independent quarter/three-quarter points. The numerical gates are max |un|/U∞ ≤0.01 and max |Cp+σ| ≤0.02 off-grid. These gates are discretization checks, not experimental accuracy.

No natural sphere detachment, tunnel walls, viscous drag, nuclei, gravity, re-entrant jet, unsteady collapse or cavitating-wing load transfer is included.
