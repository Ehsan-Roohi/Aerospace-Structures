What recent supercavitation papers let us reproduce

The Logvinovich–Semenenko lineage provides useful cavity models and measured profiles. Published equations, reported simulations and available source code carry different levels of reproducibility.

Implemented here: two finite-sample profile comparisons and one signed cone-lift fit. Research remaining: a time-dependent cavity solver, ventilation closure, contact dynamics and independently validated vehicle motion. No SCV or DIVE program has been recovered or run.

The sign of cone lift changes

The 2024 paper, Eqs. 22–23 and Tables 3–4, fits the derivative of lift to cone half-angle. Inclination is in radians; cone half-angle is in degrees. The curve below is a new algebraic evaluation of the published fit. Markers show only the reported IHM measurements, including the disk endpoint.

Paper measurementsPublished polynomial, evaluated here● Selected half-angle
f(γ) = 2 − 1.64×10⁻⁶ γ³ + 5.42×10⁻⁴ γ² − 6.65×10⁻² γ
cᵧ = f(γ) (1 + σ) δ
The zero occurs near γ = 43.425° (full cone angle 86.851°). Experiments concentrated near σ ≈ 0.03–0.04. The sliders illustrate the approximation; they do not establish validity throughout the displayed range.
A source discrepancy that affects implementation

The 2022 paper, printed p. 87 Eq. 2, prints a positive cubic term. Evaluating it at a full cone angle of 180° gives +1.60076, contrary to its plotted negative disk endpoint. The 2024 negative cubic gives −0.79036, consistent with its signed measurements. This page explicitly implements the 2024 equation; it does not silently repair the earlier paper.

Earlier leads and the next useful implementation

The 2016 maneuvering paper and 2018–2019 papers remain reference-level leads in this review: partially cavitating dynamics (2018), fin applications (2018), and maneuvering (2019). Their results have not been reproduced here.

A useful next step is a separate, versioned implementation of steady cone profiles and an unsteady section-history kernel, checked against the finite stations above before adding ventilation or contact. SCV/DIVE source, license, build instructions, input decks and raw experimental records would enable a stronger reproduction.

Scientific status: independent transcription and algebraic reproduction of published evidence. Table curves are the papers’ own measurements and calculations. No new CFD, trajectory simulation or hardware experiment is claimed. This focused review concerns cavities in water; supersonic air-cavity work is outside its scope.
