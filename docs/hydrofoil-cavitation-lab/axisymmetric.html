Method & model limits

The meridian is revolved into conical surface bands. Each band is a continuous three-dimensional source ring, not a two-dimensional airfoil solution. The azimuthal integration uses complete elliptic integrals; meridional quadrature and the exterior normal jump solve the no-penetration boundary condition.

The imposed flow is parallel to the symmetry axis. Nonzero incidence, yaw, swirl, lifting-wing effects and asymmetric cavities require a different model and are refused.

Cp = 1 − |V/U|². The sampled wet-flow pressure threshold is −min(Cp). If the predicted wet-flow pressure falls below vapour pressure, the wet-flow pressure criterion is exceeded: the actual cavity surface and its pressure redistribution have not been solved. Nuclei, viscosity, surface condition and dissolved gas are not modeled.

The displayed normal residual is the algebraic residual at panel collocation points; it is not a measure of global boundary accuracy. Independent off-collocation and exterior-field errors are reported in Validation.

Pressure drag is an ideal-flow diagnostic, not real viscous or cavitation drag. The source-only model is isolated from the 2D cavity and wing structural solvers.

Context: Alavi & Roohi (2026), Cavitation review, Part I. This review motivates separate axisymmetric geometries; its experimental cavity figures are not used here as a matched validation dataset.
