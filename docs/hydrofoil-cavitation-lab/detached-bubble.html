Detached Cavity Dynamics

Time-dependent axisymmetric boundary-element research module

Hydrofoil sheetSteady axisymmetric cavityVerification & scope

One isolated closed bubble in an unbounded liquid. This module evolves its boundary using BEM and unsteady Bernoulli. It does not simulate a hydrofoil shedding a cloud, breakup, shock waves or erosion.

Initial cavity & liquid

τ = R₀√[ρ/(p∞ − pv)]. Initial surface potential and velocity are zero. Surface tension, viscosity and gravity are omitted in this first verified case.

What is solved?

Equations, numerics & limits

The exterior liquid satisfies ∇²φ = 0 and φ → 0 at infinity. The single- and double-layer boundary integrals determine the normal velocity from the current boundary potential at every time stage.

The interface moves with the solved normal velocity. Its potential follows unsteady Bernoulli with the velocity of the moving mesh included. A second-order Heun predictor/corrector updates both geometry and potential. This is a time-marched boundary solution, not an animation of prescribed radii.

The gas pressure is pg = pg₀(V₀/V)κ, and the liquid-side interface pressure is pv + pg. Volume is integrated from the evolving conical boundary panels. No measured radius history is imposed.

Invalid geometry, nonfinite values, excessive mesh motion or an unresolved linear system stop the calculation. The last completed state is retained. Jet impact and topology change are unsupported; no clipped continuation is shown.

BIMBAMBUM (2024) demonstrates more general detached-bubble BEM. This is an independent smaller implementation, not that program. Hydrofoil cloud experiments show additional re-entrant-flow and bubbly-shock mechanisms; these are absent here.
