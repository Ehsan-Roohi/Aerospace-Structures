Scope before ranking

The paper specifies Re > 1 million, α from −4° to 12°, Mach from 0 to 0.9. ADAPT does not take Reynolds number as input. Its uncertainty does not cover low-Re model error. A 900 mm Aeroclub wing near Re 400,000 is outside this published domain.

No finite-wing induced drag, total drag, structural response or flight approval is provided here. Candidate geometries must be re-solved in Wing Lab and checked against appropriate low-Re data before design use.

Predictions use Mach 0.15. Changing Re only checks scope; it does not re-run or alter the model. The reference point is a design probe, not a computed shear centre.

Reproducibility and limitations

Pressure forces and moments are integrated over both sampled surfaces. Force/moment covariance is propagated from the full joint Cp covariance; point uncertainties are not assumed independent. Moment uses nose-up positive about the quarter-chord before transfer to the chosen reference point. Bands are pointwise, not simultaneous confidence bands, and do not include geometric or material uncertainty.

ADAPT source and weights remain in the authors' repository. This page contains our adapter and derived inference outputs, with source/weight hashes in the reproducibility record. No independent experimental validation or inverse-design optimum is claimed.
