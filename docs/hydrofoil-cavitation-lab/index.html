Numerical settings

The budget limits calculation time, not physical tolerances. A disk BEM pressure root may take several minutes at 128 bands. Cancel terminates its worker. A timed-out or failed physical trial is never displayed as an accepted cavity.

Help: inputs, output & limits

The direct problem prescribes σ = (p∞ − pv(T))/(½ρ(T)U²), or calculates it from physical flow inputs. Cavity length and shape are numerical outputs. Pressure is absolute, not gauge. The input mode has one source of truth: editing σ derives p∞; editing physical conditions derives σ.

Fresh-water saturation pressure and density use IAPWS SR1-86(1992). Saturated density approximates incompressible liquid density; no thermal phase-change, salinity, dissolved gas or compressibility model is included.

The hydrofoil driver searches the upper attached partial sheet and its endpoints. It first checks a fully wetted solution on two meshes. A no-cavity result means that this equilibrium potential-flow model does not require a cavity. The current cavitating branch remains unqualified and can fail physical screens. No length or volume is invented for failed cases.

The disk BEM solves unknown separation to an artificial Riabouchinsky rear disk. A cylindrical afterbody can be inserted only if fully dry-contained. This is not a general partly wetted cylinder, cone at incidence, or cloud-shedding model. Angle of attack is zero in the axisymmetric solver.
