Synthetic full delta wing examples
Full planform: 260 mm tip-to-tip span x 160 mm root chord; exact zero-chord tips.
Load: 5 N total upward on the symmetric full wing, chord-proportional (2.5 N per half). Self-weight is ignored in these two studies.
Both studies use NACA 0020, effective printed PLA, 0.8 mm skin, one piece, and finite integral same-material spars ending at y=65 mm.
The solver analyzes one root-clamped semi-wing; mirror it for the full symmetric geometry and loads. This does not resolve the actual centre fixture.
